Pit Commands

Start with pit stop and the service is read straight from your words: pit stop four tyres and 30 litres · pit stop wet tyres · pit stop fuel 8 gallons · pit stop fast repair · pit stop clear all. Your own phrasing (box me for wets) also works, but the model interprets it first. AIRE reads the command back: say confirm to set it, or cancel.

On: any command starting with pit stop is set at once and read back as set, with no chance to cancel a misheard one — even when the model had to interpret the wording. Commands without pit stop are always confirmed.
